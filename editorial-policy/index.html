Editorial Policy

Effective October 16, 2026Applies to all site content

This policy explains how BigBassBlast.com researches, writes, reviews and corrects its content.

Source hierarchy

  1. The information and paytable inside the exact game version reviewed.
  2. Official provider game pages, rule sheets and release announcements.
  3. Regulator or testing information where publicly available.
  4. Operator information for regional availability and operator-specific settings.
  5. Independent databases only as secondary cross-checks.

Accuracy and uncertainty

We do not treat a copied specification as verified simply because it appears on several websites. Where versions or sources disagree, we identify the difference. If a figure cannot be confirmed, we label it “not disclosed” or “not confirmed” rather than guessing.

Editorial independence

Affiliate relationships do not determine ratings, conclusions or factual claims. Commercial links are labeled where required and are kept separate from the research process.

Responsible language

We do not describe random outcomes as predictable, claim that a bonus is due, or present a strategy as capable of defeating the house edge. Maximum-win figures are not presented as typical results.

AI-assisted work

Technology may assist with drafting, formatting, consistency checks or structured data. Material claims are still reviewed against sources before publication. Automated output is not treated as a source.

Updates and corrections

Material updates receive a new fact-check or modified date. Correction requests can be sent to isac@slotessentials.com with the page URL and supporting evidence.
