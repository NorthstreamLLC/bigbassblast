Responsible Gambling

18+ onlyInformation, not treatment

Gambling should never be treated as income, an investment or a way to recover losses. Slots use random outcomes and have a long-run house advantage.

Before playing

  • Use only money left after rent, food, bills, debt payments and savings.
  • Set a fixed money limit and time limit before the first wager.
  • Do not borrow money or use credit intended for essential expenses.
  • Check whether gambling is legal and age-restricted where you are located.
  • Never use a VPN or false information to bypass restrictions.

Warning signs

  • Chasing losses or increasing stakes to recover money.
  • Hiding gambling activity or spending from other people.
  • Playing longer or spending more than intended.
  • Using gambling to escape stress, anxiety or financial pressure.
  • Missing work, responsibilities or payments because of gambling.

Use operator controls

Licensed operators may offer deposit limits, loss limits, time reminders, cooling-off periods and self-exclusion. Set limits before play and choose self-exclusion if shorter controls are not enough.

Independent support

  • GambleAware — information and support resources in Great Britain.
  • GamCare — support, live chat and the National Gambling Helpline in Great Britain.
  • Gamblers Anonymous — peer-support meetings in multiple regions.
  • National Council on Problem Gambling — United States information and support pathways.

If gambling is affecting your safety, essential finances or mental health, stop playing and contact an appropriate local support service. This website cannot provide crisis or clinical care.
