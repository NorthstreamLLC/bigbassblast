What makes Big Bass Blast special

An explosive evolution of the fishing slot formula — loaded with new mechanics and massive win potential.

Fisherman Wild

Fisherman Wilds can appear on reels one and five. When one lands with fish money symbols, it collects their displayed values; in the base game, a Wild can also carry a random multiplier from 2× to 10×.

Money Symbols

Cash-value coins scatter across all reels. When a Fisherman lands alongside them, their values combine for an instant win. Multiple Fishermen multiply the effect.

Bazooka Blast

In the bonus game, the bazooka mechanic can transform landed symbols and create another chance for money symbols and Fisherman Wilds to connect.

Hook Assist

When two Scatters land in the base game, the special hook feature can reel in a third Scatter and trigger the bonus round.

Free Spins + 10× Multiplier

3–5 Scatters trigger 5–9 Free Spins. Every seventh Wild collected awards five extra spins and raises the win multiplier from 2× to 3× and then 10×.

What is Big Bass Blast?

Big Bass Blast is a 5×3 slot released by Pragmatic Play in July 2026. Fisherman Wilds collect fish money symbols, while hook and bazooka mechanics create additional feature opportunities.

What is the RTP of Big Bass Blast?

What is the maximum win?
