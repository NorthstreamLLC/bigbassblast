Big Bass Blast symbols and paytable guide

September 20268 min readBigBassBlast.com Editorial Team

Big Bass Blast uses familiar card-value symbols, fishing-themed premiums and three symbols that control most of the feature action: the Fisherman Wild, fish Money symbols and the Scatter. Reading those roles correctly is more useful than memorising a single payout table because operators can offer different configured versions of a game.

Check the live paytable first. Open the information or help panel inside the exact demo or casino version you are using. It is the authoritative source for that session’s symbol values, paylines, stake range and theoretical return.

Regular paying symbols

The regular symbols produce line wins when enough matching symbols land on an active payline according to the in-game table. Lower-value card ranks generally pay less frequently significant amounts than the themed premium symbols. Their exact payouts can change with the number of matching symbols, so do not rely on a screenshot from another casino or currency.

Regular symbols and Money symbols are not interchangeable. A fish displaying a large value is not automatically paid merely because it appears. The collection condition shown in the game still has to occur.

The Fisherman Wild

The Fisherman Wild is both a substitute-style Wild and the collector at the centre of the Big Bass formula. Pragmatic Play states that Wilds can appear on reels one and five in the base game and may carry a random multiplier from 2x to 10x. When a collecting Wild lands with fish Money symbols, it collects their displayed values under the applicable game rules.

During free spins, Wild collection also drives progression. Every seventh collected Wild adds five spins and advances the feature multiplier—from 2x to 3x and eventually 10x. That is why the count of Fisherman Wilds matters as much as any single fish value.

Fish Money symbols

Fish Money symbols carry displayed values rather than behaving like ordinary line-paying symbols. The official game description says these values can reach 500x. A high number on the grid is potential value until the required Fisherman Wild collection happens.

Scatter, Hook, Dynamite and Bazooka

Scatter and Hook

Three, four or five Scatters trigger five, seven or nine free spins. If only two Scatters land, the Hook mechanic can add the third needed to start the bonus. It is an assist, not a guarantee every time two Scatters appear.

Dynamite and Bazooka

During the feature, Money symbols can land without a collector, or a Fisherman Wild can land without Money symbols. The Dynamite and Bazooka mechanics provide additional chances to create an instant collection outcome by bringing in or transforming relevant symbols. Their appearance remains random.

How to read the paytable responsibly

  1. Confirm the selected stake and currency before comparing monetary examples.
  2. Check which RTP configuration the operator displays; do not assume every lobby uses the same version.
  3. Review the payline diagram and the required direction for matching symbols.
  4. Separate theoretical maximums from typical outcomes.
  5. Use demo mode to learn controls, not to predict a future real-money session.

No symbol sequence, stake pattern or timing method changes an independently random spin. Set a loss limit before playing and never increase stakes to recover losses.

Symbols FAQ

What triggers the Big Bass Blast bonus?
Three to five Scatters trigger five to nine free spins. Two Scatters may activate the Hook assist, which can add a third.
Does every fish Money symbol pay?
No. It carries a potential value that must be collected under the game’s Wild-collection rules.
Where can I find exact symbol payouts?
Open the paytable or help panel in the exact version of the game you are playing. Operator configurations can differ.
