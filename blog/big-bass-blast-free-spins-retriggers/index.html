The Big Bass Blast bonus is not one flat block of spins. It is a progression feature: Scatters decide the starting number of spins, Fisherman Wild collections move the feature through multiplier stages, and every completed collection threshold adds more spins.

The Hook mechanic may add a third Scatter after two land in the base game. That is a random assist, not a result players can trigger with timing or stake changes.

The Fisherman collection ladder

During free spins, Fisherman Wilds collect the values shown on fish Money symbols. The feature also counts collected Wilds. Every seventh Wild awards five additional spins and advances the multiplier: first to 2x, then 3x, and finally 10x.

The important distinction is that a multiplier stage is earned through Wild collection. A large fish value on screen is only potential value until the feature produces a valid collection under the game rules.

Why late retriggers matter

A five-spin extension is valuable at any point, but it carries more theoretical upside after the multiplier has progressed. Five additional spins at 10x expose the session to more collection opportunities at the highest published feature multiplier. That does not make a large return likely or guaranteed; it explains why the same number of extra spins can feel different at each stage.

No spin pattern forces a retrigger. Autoplay speed, stopping the reels and changing stake cannot turn an independently random outcome into a predictable one.

Free spins versus Bonus Buy

Where available, the Bonus Buy provides direct access to the feature for a stated cost. It does not improve the published maximum, guarantee progression or remove volatility. The normal trigger and purchased trigger lead to the feature rules shown by that operator’s game version.

What to check before playing

  • Check the stake before enabling Ante Bet or purchasing a feature.
  • Read the operator-specific availability rules.
  • Set a total session limit before the feature starts.
  • Treat demo mode as mechanics practice, not a predictor of later results.

FAQ

Can free spins retrigger more than once?
The collection ladder can award additional five-spin extensions as its published thresholds are reached. Confirm the exact rules in the version you open.
Does buying the bonus improve the odds inside it?
A purchase changes how you enter the feature. It should not be treated as a guarantee of a profitable feature or multiplier progression.
