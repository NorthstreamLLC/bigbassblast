What Ante Bet changes

At a $1 base stake, a 50% Ante Bet makes each spin cost $1.50. You continue playing the base game while paying more per attempt for the altered Scatter chance shown in the game. Because every spin costs more, the bankroll can decline faster even before the bonus appears.

What Bonus Buy changes

At a $1 base bet, a 100x purchase costs $100 and moves directly to the feature. It removes the waiting period but not the game’s volatility. A purchased bonus can return less than its cost, and the feature does not have to reach a higher multiplier stage.

Compare cost using one budget

A $100 budget funds 100 ordinary $1 spins, about 66 spins at $1 plus a 50% Ante Bet, or one 100x feature purchase. Those are three very different exposure patterns. None changes the fact that outcomes remain random and the theoretical return is measured over very large samples.

Check the actual game panel. Optional modes can carry different RTP values or costs. This article describes the reviewed configuration, not every operator version.

Which option fits a learning session?

For understanding paylines, Hook behaviour, symbols and controls, ordinary demo spins are the clearest starting point. Ante Bet is useful only for learning how the displayed stake changes. Bonus Buy demonstrates the feature quickly, but it can hide how much of the normal game happens outside free spins.

Practical checklist

  • Confirm the final stake after Ante Bet is enabled.
  • Translate the Bonus Buy multiplier into money before confirming.
  • Verify the RTP shown for the selected mode.
  • Never increase the stake to recover a previous purchase.
  • Use only an operator and feature legally available in your location.
