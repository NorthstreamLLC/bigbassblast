Big Bass Blast vs Big Bass Bonanza

Both games use the Big Bass fishing theme, but their collection and bonus progression differ. This comparison covers the original Big Bass Bonanza, not Bonanza 1000 or another sequel. Both games have bonus multipliers; the distinction is how they build.

Key differences

Collection timing: Blast's fishermen collect fish money symbols in both base and bonus play. The original Bonanza's published rules describe money collection during free spins. Blast's base-game fishermen can also carry a random multiplier up to 10×.

Bonus entry: Three to five scatters award five to nine free spins in Blast, versus ten, fifteen or twenty in original Bonanza. Blast also has a hook that can add the missing third scatter when two land.

Retrigger progression: Blast grants five extra spins for each seventh wild, while Bonanza grants ten for each fourth wild. Both progress through 2×, 3× and 10× bonus multipliers. The thresholds alone do not tell you which feature is more likely to pay more; that also depends on the game's full probability model.

Additional features: Blast's bonus includes dynamite or bazooka opportunities when fish symbols or fishermen appear without their collecting counterpart. These are random features, not controls that players can reliably activate.
