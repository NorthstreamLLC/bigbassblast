Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

This policy explains the limited information that may be processed when you visit BigBassBlast.com.

The site uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic and page usage. We use this information to maintain and improve the site.

Cookies

Analytics and third-party services may store cookies or similar identifiers. Browser controls can be used to block or remove cookies, although doing so may affect some features.

Email

If you contact us, we receive the email address and information you choose to send. Do not send passwords, identity documents or financial credentials. Correspondence may be retained as reasonably necessary to respond, document a correction or meet legal obligations.

Affiliate and external links

Outbound sites have their own privacy policies. An affiliate link may contain a referral identifier so the destination can attribute a qualifying action. We do not control how third-party websites process information.

Data choices

You can restrict cookies through browser settings and avoid sending optional personal information. Privacy questions can be sent to isac@slotessentials.com.

Changes

We may update this policy when the site's technology or legal obligations change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
