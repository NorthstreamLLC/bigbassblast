Overview

Big Bass Blast is a 2026 entry in Pragmatic Play and Reel Kingdom's Big Bass series. It keeps the Fisherman and fish Money symbol collection mechanic while adding Hook and Bazooka assists.

The game uses a 5×3 grid with 10 fixed paylines. Its 5,000× maximum win and high-volatility label indicate that results can be uneven, with extended losing periods possible. The advertised maximum does not describe how likely that outcome is.

Features in detail

Fisherman Wild & Money Symbols

The Fisherman Wild can appear on reels one and five. When it lands with fish Money symbols, it collects their displayed values. In the base game, a Fisherman Wild can also carry a random multiplier from 2× to 10×. Check the paytable in the exact game version because operator configurations can differ.

Fish Money symbols display values tied to the selected stake. Their values are collected only when the feature conditions shown in the game rules are met.

Bazooka & Hook Assist

The Hook can reel in a third Scatter after two Scatters land in the base game. Pragmatic Play describes the Bazooka as a bonus-game assist that can transform landed symbols and create another chance for Money symbols and Fisherman Wilds to connect.

Free Spins

Three, four or five Scatters trigger five, seven or nine Free Spins. Every seventh Wild collected during the feature awards five additional spins and advances the multiplier to 2×, then 3× and then 10×. The overall result remains random and the published maximum is not a typical or expected return.

Bonus Buy & Ante Bet

Where available, players can purchase direct access to the Free Spins feature. An Ante Bet option can also change the stake and scatter chance. Confirm the exact cost, RTP and feature rules in the information panel for the version offered by your operator. See our dedicated Bonus Buy guide.
