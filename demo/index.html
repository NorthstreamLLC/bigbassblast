How to use the Big Bass Blast demo

The demo is best used to learn the interface and recognise features—not to forecast results.

Choose a virtual stake, press spin and use the paytable or information button inside the game to confirm the rules for the version currently loaded. Watch for Fisherman Wilds on reels one and five: when a Wild lands with fish money symbols, it collects their displayed values. In the base game, a Fisherman Wild can also carry a random 2×–10× multiplier.

Three to five Scatters start the bonus with five to nine Free Spins. During that feature, every seventh Wild collected adds five spins and raises the multiplier through 2×, 3× and 10×. Pragmatic Play also describes special hook and bazooka mechanics that can create extra opportunities for Scatters, money symbols or Wilds.
